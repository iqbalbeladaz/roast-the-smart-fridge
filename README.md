## The entry

> My smart fridge logs every midnight snack, then greets me each morning with a salad recipe — a roommate who saw everything and forgives nothing.

144 characters, one line, one sentence. The roast names two real
smart-fridge behaviours — inventory tracking and recipe suggestions —
and turns the fridge into a judgmental roommate who saw everything and
forgives nothing, instead of the stock joke about a fridge ordering too
much milk.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) writes the line to
`/output/entry.txt`, refusing to write anything outside the gate:
1–160 characters, a single non-empty line, a single sentence.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
